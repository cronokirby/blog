(2026-08) Upper bounds for failure probabilities of reductions from low density subset sum problems to lattice problems on linearly independent vectors

  • Shoichi Kamada
2026-08-11
  • #cryptography
  • #paper

Abstract

As a new lattice problem, we introduce lll-Shortest Independent Vectors Problem (lll-SIVP for short), where lll is a positive integer no greater than the rank of a lattice. In the case where l=1l=1l=1, lll-SIVP means SVP, and in the case where lll is the rank of a lattice, lll-SIVP means SIVP. We estimate upper bounds on the failure probabilities of the reductions from the subset sum problems to the lll-SIVPs in terms of Ehrhart theory. Especially, in the case of l=1l=1l=1, our upper bound is tighter than the previous result given by Coster et al. We give some considerations for dominating terms of our upper bounds of failure probabilities when lll is general.
