Abstract

We present a practical framework DumboMix\mathsf{DumboMix}DumboMix for asynchronous anonymous broadcasts with guaranteed output delivery (G.O.D., a.k.a. robustness), enabling a set of nnn servers to privately solicit NNN messages from distinct clients, such that these messages remain secret until they are simultaneously revealed in a uniformly random order. Here, asynchronous G.O.D. ensures that all solicited messages will eventually be randomly mixed despite (i) arbitrary malicious behaviors by up to n/3n/3n/3 Byzantine servers and (ii) unpredictable network delays and jitters.

At the core of DumboMix\mathsf{DumboMix}DumboMix, we first propose a couple of practical arithmetic circuits DumboMix1\mathsf{DumboMix1}DumboMix1 and DumboMix2\mathsf{DumboMix2}DumboMix2 for mixing in Shamir-secret-shared multi-party computation (MPC) over Zp\mathbb{Z}_pZp​, along with their server-optimized variants. Moreover, assuming a robust underlying MPC framework, they guarantee that all revealed inputs are uniformly shuffled. In contrast, existing techniques fail to achieve all these performance and functionality features: The DC-net variant Blinder\mathsf{Blinder}Blinder (CCS’20) may reveal a non-negligible fraction of inputs without shuffling them; Butterfly switching networks in secret-shared MPC (CCS’19) incur O(\log2N)\mathcal{O}(\log^2 N)O(log2N) multiplicative depth; RabbitMix (Security’24) requires O(N2)\mathcal{O}(N^2)O(N2) MPC multiplications; and PowerMix (CCS’19) incurs N3/2N^{3}/2N3/2 scalar multiplications.
